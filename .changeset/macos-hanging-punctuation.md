日本語の行末の句読点を行の外に配置する、いわゆる「ぶら下げ」ができるようになりました。句読点の前の字が次の行に送られず、行の長さが揃って読みやすくなります。  
このレイアウトが執筆の集中を妨げる場合は、設定の「行末の句読点をぶら下げる」オプションでオフにできます。

Japanese commas and periods at the end of a line can now extend into the margin, a technique known as hanging punctuation. The character before them is no longer pushed to the next line, so line lengths stay even and your writing is easier to read.  
If you find this layout distracting while you write, you can turn off Hang Punctuation at Line Ends in Settings.
