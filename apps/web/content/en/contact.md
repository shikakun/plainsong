For inquiries about Plainsong, please write to [plainsong@shikakun.com](mailto:plainsong@shikakun.com).

Please note that replies are written in Japanese only, though you are welcome to write in English. Plainsong is developed by one person, so a reply may take some time.

## Bug Reports

Including the following makes it easier to reproduce the problem.

- Your macOS version
- Your Plainsong version (see "Plainsong" > "About Plainsong" in the menu bar)
- The steps that lead to the problem
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened (including the text of any error message)
