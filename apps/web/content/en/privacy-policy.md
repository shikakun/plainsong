Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes how information is handled in the macOS application "Plainsong" (the "App").

## Summary

The App does not collect or transmit any user data, personal or otherwise.

The App is a fully local application. No account is required, it contains no analytics, no behavioral tracking, and no advertising, and what you write never leaves your Mac.

## Data the App Handles and Where It Is Stored

All data the App reads and writes is stored only on your Mac's local storage.

| Data | Location |
|---|---|
| Drafts | Inside the app's container (`~/Library/Containers/com.shikakun.plainsong/`) |
| Files you open or save | Wherever you choose |
| Autosave backups | Inside the app's container |
| Imported themes | Inside the app's container |
| App settings | Inside the app's container |

None of this data is ever received by the developer or any third party.

## File Access

The App runs in the macOS App Sandbox and only accesses files you choose.

## Network Communication

The App does not communicate over the network. When you use Share in the toolbar, the text is passed to the destination you choose.

## What Is Not Collected

- Account information (there is no account system at all)
- Usage or behavioral logs (no telemetry or analytics tools are built into the App)
- Advertising identifiers (the App shows no ads)
- Location, contacts, or any other sensitive information

## Statistics the Developer May Receive

Apple provides the developer with anonymized, aggregated statistics such as download counts. In addition, for users who have opted in to sharing with app developers via macOS settings (Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple also provides aggregated usage data, such as the number of sessions, and crash reports. Both are functions of Apple's App Store, not something the App collects itself. The developer cannot identify any individual from these anonymized statistics.

## Information Received Through Inquiries

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address and the content of your inquiry. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, is not used for any other purpose, and is not shared with third parties.

## Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App collects no user data, there is no data to share with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

The App collects no data from users of any age.

## For Users Outside Japan (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

The App does not collect, process, or store personal data. Accordingly, as a rule there is no data held by the developer that could be subject to access, deletion, or other requests under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or similar laws. For information received in the course of responding to inquiries (such as your email address), you can request access, correction, or deletion via the details on the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.

## About the Website

The App's website ([https://plainsong.shikakun.com/](https://plainsong.shikakun.com/), including the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages) uses the following services to understand how the site is used. These apply to the website only; the App itself still collects and transmits nothing.

- **Google Analytics** (provided by Google LLC): uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze browsing activity such as pages viewed, referrers, browser type, and approximate region. Collected information may be processed on Google's servers, including in the United States. For how Google handles this data, see "[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)". You can opt out using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by disabling cookies in your browser.
- **Cloudflare analytics** (provided by Cloudflare, Inc.): the website is served through Cloudflare, which processes access logs (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site and to aggregate visit counts. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.
